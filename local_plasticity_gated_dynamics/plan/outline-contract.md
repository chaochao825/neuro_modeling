# Outline Contract

## Paper spine

The paper asks one question: when an unlabelled personalized-video stream is
processed causally, does prediction concentration measure operator
reliability, or does it mistake stable errors for competence and obscure a
simpler temporal-aggregation explanation?

This is an audit paper. It does not validate the full Actuator Matching
programme and does not introduce a successful new router.

## Contribution wording allowed today

1. A matched-tape causal audit across heterogeneous few-shot operators,
   cumulative voting, probability accumulation, validation-only calibration,
   lagged selection, and fixed temporal control.
2. A formal and controlled demonstration that prediction concentration does
   not identify correctness: the stable-wrong operator locks the consistency
   selector with wrong-lock fraction one.
3. A complete retrospective ORBIT result in which prefix consistency is 5.99
   percentage points below equal prefix probability accumulation at the user
   level, with all failed, invalid, and opposing evidence retained.
4. An explicitly exploratory observation that within-operator prefix
   probability accumulation produces large gains, while incremental
   heterogeneous-bank value remains inconclusive.

- compute efficiency or sparse execution;
- participating E/I execution or neural validation;
