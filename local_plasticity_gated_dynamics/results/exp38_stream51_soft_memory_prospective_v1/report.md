# Exp38 Stream-51 soft-memory qualification report

## Outcome

**Main claim: INCONCLUSIVE. Registered qualification: OPPOSED (0/5 seeds passed the joint gate).**

The external stage remains locked. No external embedding, prediction, label fit, or outcome was generated or inspected. This is a fail-closed result, not a negative external comparison.

## What held

- The independent support/development cache audit passed: 755 source videos, 34,250 frames, 1,280-dimensional finite features, zero video failures, and zero external rows.
- Oracle cumulative memory with perfect switch reset exceeded the stronger fixed time scale in 5/5 seeds (headroom range 0.0273 to 0.0558).
- Cumulative memory was strongly harmed after hidden switches in 5/5 seeds (registered harm range 0.7115 to 0.7356).
- Every selected controller was eligible on the development-fit partition; causal risk AUC was 0.746 to 0.831.

## Why qualification failed

- Stable accumulation cleared the 0.02 MCID in 2/5 seeds. The other three produced 0.01952, missing the frozen threshold by 0.00048.
- Switch reachability cleared the joint recall/false-alarm/delay gate in only 1/5 seeds. Recall ranged 0.172 to 0.359; seed 13803 also exceeded the false-alarm ceiling.
- Consequently no seed satisfied all gates. The all-seed rule prohibits external access.

## Descriptive controller utility on qualification

This was not a substitute external endpoint. Soft retention minus the stronger fixed forgetting/window baseline averaged +0.0019 across assembly seeds (range -0.0038 to +0.0077), far below the registered external MCID of 0.02.

## Interpretation

The new Stream-51 task does expose a real need to combine stable accumulation with switch-dependent forgetting. What failed is the robust conversion of the three-dimensional causal statistic into a reliable actuator across stream assemblies. High AUC with low fixed-threshold recall indicates ranking information without sufficiently stable causal actuation. Under the frozen stop rule, this adaptive-memory line is not advanced as a paper-method claim and is not rescued by post-hoc threshold tuning.

## Execution audit

- Attempt 1 stopped before embeddings because the schema validator initially rejected official tracker boxes that the reference loader clips; its log is retained.
- Attempt 2 cached 90 videos before an outcome-blind ordered-decode speed amendment. A surviving child writer caused a manifest race, was detected and stopped, and its zero-byte temporary file was staged in ignored trash rather than deleted.
- Attempt 3 completed all 755 registered support/development videos. The final manifest and every feature file were independently revalidated before qualification.
- All five qualification seeds and failures are retained.
